## Overview

Partnered with a large global technology company to scale an AI accelerator for enterprise call center agents, streamlining build workflows and reducing time-to-deployment for agentic solutions.

## Key Achievements

### Cross-Functional Leadership
- Led execution across 20+ stakeholders and 3 teams
- Drove meeting cadence and tracked action items
- Improved end-to-end visibility into agent build progress and performance readiness

### Executive Communication
- Delivered executive-ready reporting on agent development
- Surfaced capability gaps and performance insights
- Informed product releases and ensured alignment on launch readiness

## Impact

This initiative resulted in significantly reduced time-to-deployment for agentic solutions and improved coordination across teams, enabling the company to accelerate its AI agent capabilities.
