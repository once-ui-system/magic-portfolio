## Overview

Built 3 multi-stage econometric models in Python to assess how GDP, unemployment, and business conditions shape annual U.S. venture capital investment.

## Research Methodology

### Model Development
- Constructed 3 multi-stage econometric models in Python
- Analyzed GDP, unemployment, and business condition variables
- Incorporated macroeconomic indicators and market conditions

### Key Finding
**A 1% Fed Funds Rate increase cuts annual U.S. venture capital investment by $1.1B**

## Policy Impact

### Federal Reserve Recommendations
- Analysis supported a **25-50 bps Fed Funds Rate cut recommendation** for the upcoming FOMC meeting
- Provided quantified evidence of monetary policy transmission to venture capital markets

## Significance

This research demonstrated the material impact of Federal Reserve policy decisions on venture capital investment, informing policy discussions at the highest levels of central banking and financial markets.
