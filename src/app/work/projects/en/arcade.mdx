# Arcade - Prediction Market

**Arcade** is a high-fidelity, decentralized prediction market Proof of Concept (PoC) custom-built on the **Arc Testnet (Circle L1)**. The platform features a unique cyberpunk dark mode aesthetic and utilizes a Constant Product Market Maker (CPMM) to ensure continuous liquidity for prediction shares.

## Key Features

- **Automated Market Maker (AMM):** Implements a Constant Product Market Maker (CPMM) algorithm to calculate odds and offer continuous trading liquidity for YES/NO outcomes.
- **Circle L1 Integration:** Built natively for the Arc Testnet, utilizing USDC as the primary collateral currency for predicting.
- **High-Fidelity Cyberpunk UI:** Custom design system utilizing Rajdhani and Inter typography with a dark mode-only theme (Void Black and Neon Cyan) built using Framer Motion and GSAP.
- **Factory Contract Architecture:** Utilizes a Factory pattern to allow permissionless creation of new prediction markets for real-world events.

## Architecture & Technology Stack

The platform is split into three main components:

### 1. Smart Contracts
The core logic resides in Solidity smart contracts deployed on Arc.
* **PredictionMarket.sol:** Manages pool liquidity, share trading, collateral staking (USDC), and outcome resolution.
* **MarketFactory.sol:** Standardizes and automates prediction market deployment.
* **Testing & Tools:** 100% test coverage using Foundry (unit tests, integration tests, and fuzzing for AMM invariants).

### 2. Indexer
Tracks on-chain events emitted by the Factory and individual prediction pools to provide fast queries to the frontend.
* **Tech Stack:** Node.js, Custom Event Indexer.

### 3. Frontend Application
A responsive, high-performance web interface designed for real-time portfolio tracking and seamless wallet connection.
* **Tech Stack:** Next.js, Framer Motion, GSAP, TailwindCSS.
