## Overview

Analyzed a smart-charging EV program for a green energy startup by building a difference-in-differences model in R, identifying peak-hour grid optimization opportunities and quantifying savings.

## Research Design

### Analytical Approach
- Built difference-in-differences model in R
- Analyzed 600-vehicle smart charging dataset
- Evaluated peak-hour (6-9pm) grid behavior

### Key Finding
**$2.04 average daily savings per vehicle** through smart charging optimization during peak hours

## Grid Optimization

### Peak-Hour Analysis
- Focused on critical 6-9pm demand period
- Identified load-shifting opportunities
- Quantified economic and environmental benefits

### Impact
- Demonstrated ROI of smart charging technology
- Provided data-driven recommendations for grid optimization
- Supported green energy startup's business case

## Significance

This analysis provided quantified evidence of smart charging technology's value in grid optimization and customer savings, supporting both environmental and economic objectives.
