## Meta tags

Magic Portfolio automatically generates meta- and open graph tags for your pages based on the `content.js` file.

## Schema

Magic Portfolio automatically generates schema tags for your pages based on the `content.js` file.

Every page also carries the site's structured data: the website, and the person (or, with `schema.type: "Organization"`, the organization) from the `schema` and `sameAs` settings in `once-ui.config.ts`. Posts and projects point to them by a stable `@id` and add a breadcrumb trail, the word count and the last-modified date.

## Canonical URLs and the sitemap

Each page declares its canonical URL, built from `baseURL`. The sitemap dates every post and project by `updatedAt` (or `publishedAt`), and the home, blog and work pages by their newest entry.

## Search Console

To verify your site in Google Search Console with the meta tag method, set `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` in your `.env` file to the code Google gives you.

## Open Graph image

Social sharing images (open-graph and twitter) are automatically generated with `next/og`.
