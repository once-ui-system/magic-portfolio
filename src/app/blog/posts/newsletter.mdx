The newsletter block on your home page and blog sends every signup straight into your [Aveiro](https://www.aveiro.app) audience. Aveiro is where you then write, send and measure the newsletter itself, so there is no list to export or sync.

## Create an embed form

Sign up for [Aveiro](https://www.aveiro.app), open **Audience → Embed** and create a form. Add your portfolio's domain to the form's allowed sites, then copy the form's public ID.

## Connect it to your portfolio

Paste the public ID into the `newsletterForm` object. Signups go to Aveiro directly from the page, with no new tab and no redirect.

## Newsletter content

You can change the headline and description in `content.tsx`, or hide the block with `display: false`.

<CodeBlock
    highlight="2"
    codes={[
  {
    code:
`const newsletter: Newsletter = {
  display: true,
  title: <>Subscribe to {person.firstName}'s Newsletter</>,
  description: <>My weekly newsletter about creativity and engineering</>,
};`,
    language: "tsx",
    label: "src/resources/content.tsx"
  }
]} />

## Background effect

The block's background is set in the same `newsletterForm` object. Turn the gradient, dots, lines and grid on or off and tune how they look.
